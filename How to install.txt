How to install Leftovers

1. Drag Leftovers into the Applications folder (in this window).
2. Open Leftovers from Applications.
   macOS will say it "can't be opened" (it isn't from the App Store).
   Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway".
   You only need to do this once.
3. Click "Allow" when Leftovers asks to send notifications.

A takeout box with chopsticks appears in your menu bar.
  No dot      all clear
  Orange dot  something needs a look
  Red dot     something is stuck

Click it to see what Claude left running. Nothing is ever stopped without your click.
Needs macOS 14 or newer and the Claude desktop app.
